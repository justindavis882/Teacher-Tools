Privacy & Terms of Use — Teacher Browser Tools

Revised on 11/22/2026

How Your Data Is Handled

Teacher Browser Tools is designed to run locally in your web browser without account logins. Data you enter—such as notes, student/activity lists, seating layouts, or QR text—stays in your browser session or local browser storage/exports rather than being collected on a central server by the creator.

Protecting Student Information (PII)

Please avoid entering sensitive Personally Identifiable Information (PII) or protected student records (such as full names paired with IDs, grades, accommodations, or personal contact details). Best practice is to use first names, initials, or group labels. Because data lives on the device and screen in use, teachers should clear session data on shared devices and be mindful of what is visible during screen sharing or projection.

Device Permissions (Mic & Camera)

If a tool requests microphone access (for volume feedback) or camera access (for scanning), processing occurs locally in your browser to power that feature and is not recorded or uploaded by this site.

Terms of Use

You and your school or district are solely responsible for ensuring your classroom use complies with applicable school policies and student privacy laws. External links or QR codes generated by users may point to third-party sites outside the creator's control.
